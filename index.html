PART II · THE CLIMB

YOU CAME BACK.

The climb is over.

You fell more times than you can remember, and every time, you got back up. You kept moving even when every step felt heavier than the last. You were afraid that trying would never be enough, that one day someone would decide you had become too much to carry.

But you kept trying anyway.

You came back to the places you thought you had left behind. You saw pieces of memories you thought were gone. You found the same doors, the same rooms, the same pieces of a life you were still learning how to understand.

Maybe moving forward was never about forgetting.

Maybe it was never about becoming someone who never falls.

Maybe it was simply about getting back up.

The path ahead is still there.

You are not finished.

But for the first time, you are no longer running from where you came from.

You came back.

There is still something waiting for you.
